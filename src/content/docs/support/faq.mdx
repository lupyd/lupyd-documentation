## General

### What is Lupyd?

Lupyd is a secure platform that combines social interaction, content creation, and
business connectivity, with a strong focus on privacy and encryption.

### Is Lupyd free?

Lupyd is free to use, with optional premium features. See
[pricing](https://billing.lupyd.com).

### How do I create an account?

Install the app (see [Installation](/getting-started/installation/)) and follow the
sign-up flow.

## Privacy and security

### Does Lupyd sell user data?

No. Lupyd does not sell or share user data with third parties.

### Are my messages secure?

Yes. Direct messages are end-to-end encrypted with the Signal protocol and group
messages with the [MLS protocol](/developers/firefly/encryption/). Lupyd's servers only
relay encrypted data and cannot read your messages.

### Who can see my profile?

You control your visibility under [privacy settings](/guides/settings/).

## Features and usage

### Can I use Lupyd for business?

Yes. Lupyd supports both individuals and businesses (B2B and B2C).

### Which devices are supported?

Android, iOS, Windows, Linux, macOS, and the web. You can use up to five devices on one
account at the same time.

## Support

Need help with your account, messaging, group chats, or app performance?

- Use **Settings → Help and support** in the app to report a problem or contact support.
- Check [platform troubleshooting](/guides/platforms/#troubleshooting) for quick fixes.
