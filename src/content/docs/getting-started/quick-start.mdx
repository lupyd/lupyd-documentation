Start building your presence on Lupyd in minutes: set up, publish, and go live.

## Build and launch

<Steps>

1. **Create your profile.** Add essentials such as your name, category, and contact
   information. Your profile is your storefront on Lupyd.
2. **Set up your identity.** Add a clear profile photo, a short bio, and the right
   category so people instantly understand what you offer.
3. **Publish your first post.** Introduce yourself, your business, product, or service.
   Keep it simple, clear, and visually engaging.
4. **Customise your presence.** Keep visuals and branding consistent to build trust and
   recognition.
5. **Create your offer.** Highlight what you provide — products, services, or
   experiences — and make it easy to act on.
6. **Go live.** Your profile can now be discovered, and people can engage with it.
7. **Connect and expand.** Add external links and contact options so people can reach
   you beyond Lupyd.
8. **Track early activity.** Watch how people interact with your profile and use what
   you learn to plan your next steps.

</Steps>

## Grow your audience

| Strategy | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Get discovered | Use relevant categories and [hashtags](/guides/features/), clear titles, and engaging visuals. |
| Post with purpose | Give every post a goal — attract, inform, or convert. |
| Hook attention fast | Lead with strong visuals, short captions, and a clear message. |
| Stay consistent | Post regularly so your profile stays active and familiar. |
| Engage actively | Reply to comments and take part in conversations. |
| Learn what works | Repeat the formats that perform best. |
| Collaborate | Partner with other creators or businesses to reach new audiences. |
| Share beyond Lupyd | Promote your profile on other platforms. |
| Optimise continuously | Refine your profile and content based on feedback. |

<Aside type="caution" title="Common mistakes">
	- Posting without a clear goal
	- Ignoring audience interaction
	- Inconsistent activity
	- Low-quality visuals
</Aside>

Growth is ongoing: experiment, adapt, and keep improving.
