Lupyd is available on all major platforms — Android, iOS, Windows, Linux, and macOS — and
the onboarding experience is the same everywhere. There is no platform-specific setup.

## Install from an app store

<Steps>

1. Open your device's app store.
2. Search for **Lupyd**.
3. Tap **Install**.
4. Launch the application.
5. Create an account or sign in.

</Steps>

No further configuration is required.

## Download directly

Official desktop and mobile builds are also available from the downloads page.

<LinkButton href="https://download.lupyd.com" icon="external" iconPlacement="end">
	Go to downloads
</LinkButton>

## What you get out of the box

- **One interface everywhere** — the same layout and features on mobile and desktop.
- **Secure sign-in** — your account works across all of your devices.
- **Real-time sync** — messages and content stay in sync between devices.
- **Encryption by default** — end-to-end encryption is enabled automatically; there is
  nothing to turn on.

<Aside type="tip">
	Each device you sign in on is registered separately so that it gets its own encryption
	keys. You can use up to five devices at once; signing in on a sixth replaces the device
	you used least recently. Review and remove devices in your account settings.
</Aside>
