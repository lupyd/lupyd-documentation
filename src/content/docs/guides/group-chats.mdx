Lupyd group chats bring people together through secure, real-time communication —
for casual conversations, team collaboration, or business discussions.

Every group is end-to-end encrypted using the [Messaging Layer Security (MLS)
protocol](/developers/firefly/encryption/). Messages are encrypted on your device and can
only be decrypted by current group members; Lupyd's servers relay ciphertext and never
see message contents.

## Key capabilities

### Create and manage groups

- Create a group with multiple participants.
- Add or remove members.
- Assign roles (admin or member).
- Customise the group name and profile.

### Secure group messaging

- End-to-end encrypted conversations.
- Messages are readable only by group members.
- No data is shared with third parties.

### Calls and scheduling

- Group voice and video calls with encrypted sessions.
- Schedule calls or discussions in advance, with reminders sent to participants.

### Media sharing

- Share images, videos, and files within the group.

### Multi-device sync

- Each of your devices joins the group as its own MLS member, so conversations stay in
  sync between mobile and desktop.

### Notifications

- Real-time notifications for messages and calls, with per-group controls.

<Aside type="note" title="Membership changes rotate keys">
	When someone joins or leaves a group, the group's encryption keys are updated. Removed
	members cannot read messages sent after they left. New members only see earlier
	messages if the group has history sharing enabled, in which case existing members
	share an encrypted copy of the history with them.
</Aside>

## Privacy and trust

- Conversations are never exposed outside the group.
- Interactions are not tracked or sold.
- Strong encryption keeps conversations confidential.

## Use cases

- **Personal groups** — stay connected with friends and family.
- **Business collaboration** — coordinate with teams and schedule meetings in one place.
- **Communities** — build and manage communities with structured group communication.
