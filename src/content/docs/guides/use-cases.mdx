Lupyd is for individuals, creators, and businesses who want to build a presence, share
content, and connect with an audience in one place.

## Businesses

Create a digital presence, showcase offerings, and connect with customers.

- Create a business profile.
- Post product updates and offers.
- Share visuals that attract customers.
- Engage directly with your audience.

**Outcome:** more visibility, better engagement, and more potential customers.

## Content creators

Share content, grow an audience, and build a personal brand.

- Post videos, images, and updates.
- Keep a consistent content flow.
- Engage with followers.
- Expand reach through discovery.

**Outcome:** audience growth and a stronger personal brand.

## Service providers

Freelancers and professionals present services and attract clients.

- Showcase services clearly.
- Share past work.
- Add contact options and respond to enquiries.

**Outcome:** more leads and direct business opportunities.

## Promotions and campaigns

Brands promote campaigns, offers, and announcements.

- Launch promotional posts.
- Highlight discounts or new releases.
- Drive engagement through content.

**Outcome:** better campaign reach and response.

## Combining use cases

Many people combine these — a business that also creates content, a creator promoting
services, or a brand running campaigns while building a community. Lupyd brings content
creation, business discovery, and audience engagement together, reducing the need for
multiple tools.
