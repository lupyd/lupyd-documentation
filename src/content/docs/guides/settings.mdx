Manage your account, control your experience, and customise how you use Lupyd.

| Section | What you can do | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Profile** | Edit name, username, and profile photo; update bio and category; change business details. | Keep your identity accurate and up to date. |
| **Account and security** | Change password; enable two-factor authentication; manage signed-in devices. | Protect your account and control access. |
| **Notifications** | Control push notifications, email/SMS preferences, and activity alerts. | Stay informed without being overwhelmed. |
| **Privacy** | Set profile visibility; control who can contact you; manage content visibility. | Decide who sees your content and interacts with you. |
| **Business** | Set business category and tags, contact details, and product or service information. | Help people discover and connect with your business. |
| **Payments and monetisation** | Add payment details, track earnings, and manage subscriptions. | Control how you earn and get paid. |
| **Appearance** | Choose light or dark theme, layout, and language. | Personalise your experience. |
| **Connected accounts** | Link or disconnect external platforms and integrations. | Extend your reach beyond Lupyd. |
| **Activity and data** | View account activity, download your data, and see usage insights. | Stay in control of your data. |
| **Help and support** | Browse FAQs, report a problem, or contact support. | Get help when you need it. |
| **Account management** | Deactivate or delete your account. | Full control over your account lifecycle. |

## Devices

Every device you sign in on is registered with its own encryption keys. Removing a device
from **Account and security** signs it out and removes it from your encrypted
conversations.
