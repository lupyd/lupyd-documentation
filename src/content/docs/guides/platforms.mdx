Lupyd offers the same core features on every platform, with each client optimised for
its device. Start with the [installation guide](/getting-started/installation/) if you
haven't installed Lupyd yet.

<Tabs syncKey="platform">
	<TabItem label="Mobile" icon="phone">
		**Getting started**

		- Install Lupyd from your app store.
		- Sign in or create an account.
		- Open your personalised home feed.

		**Key actions**

		- Create and publish content.
		- Explore trending posts and creators.
		- Send and receive encrypted messages.
		- Discover businesses and services.

		**Navigation** — use the bottom navigation bar to switch between features.
	</TabItem>
	<TabItem label="Web and desktop" icon="laptop">
		**Access**

		- Open Lupyd in your browser or the desktop app.
		- Sign in with your account.

		**Key actions**

		- Manage content and interactions.
		- Use messaging and business features.
		- Work in a wider dashboard view.

		**Advantages** — a larger workspace, keyboard and mouse navigation, and multitasking.
	</TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Navigation

- **Sidebar** — reach every main section; collapse it for focus.
- **Search** — find content, people, or businesses.
- **Profile and settings** — manage account details, preferences, and
  [privacy](/guides/settings/).

## Notifications

- Real-time updates for messages and interactions.
- Engagement updates for your content.
- Configurable notification preferences.

## Sync across devices

- Use Lupyd on several devices at once.
- Messages and content sync in real time.
- Switch between mobile and desktop seamlessly.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Try this |
| --- | --- |
| Can't sign in | Check your connection, then sign out and back in. |
| App is slow | Restart the app and make sure you are on the latest version. |
| Messages not syncing | Check your connection and restart the app. If it persists, remove and re-add the device in settings. |

Still stuck? See [Support](/support/faq/#support).
